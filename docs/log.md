## Creator Notes (from README)

- **Fourth installment** of HAL's original character creation series
- Character from **Bakumatsu Roman Dai Ni Maku - Gekka no Kenshi ~Tsuki ni Saku Hana, Chiri Yuku Hana~** (The Last Blade 2 - Samurai Spirits)
- HAL wanted to make a female character and chose Kojiroh (younger sister); Yukihime was another candidate
- Ogaki-san remade Kojiroh's older brother — both Kojirohs can be tried together
- More troublesome processes than expected, but close to original specifications achieved
